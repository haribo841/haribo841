# Adam Kubiś

M.Sc.Eng. in Electronics and Telecommunications, Poznan University of Technology (2026). At the [Institute of Multimedia Telecommunications](https://www.multimedia.edu.pl/), I contribute to research and implementation work, scientific publications, and teaching support.

I build software at the intersection of multimedia, digital signal processing, telecommunications, and education. My main tools are C# and .NET, C++, Python, JavaScript and Node.js, Avalonia UI, and SQLite.
